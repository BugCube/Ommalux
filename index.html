About Ommalux

Ommalux brings live camera viewing, image acquisition and camera diagnostics to Android. Connect a compatible USB UVC camera, including a microscope or webcam, and configure your imaging workflow in one place. Optional Device Camera Fallback lets you use a supported built-in camera when a USB camera is unavailable.

Photo, Video and Timelapse

Capture individual photos, record video or acquire timed image sequences. Choose PNG, TIF or JPEG for photos and supported MP4 or AVI options for video. Camera-original JPEG and H.264 MP4 preserve the camera's compressed output where supported. A lossy compression stage indicator helps explain the selected acquisition path.

Camera Controls

Adjust the controls your camera provides, including exposure, gain, focus, zoom, white balance and image adjustments. Assign supported controls to the live Adjust bar and save camera profiles for repeatable configurations. Available controls differ between USB and built-in cameras.

Preview and Alignment

Use digital zoom and alignment overlays, including central crosshairs and composition grids. Preview overlays are excluded from saved images and videos. Choose from multiple interface styles, including Standard Metallic Blue.

Storage and Workflow

Save acquisitions to device storage, a selected folder or a configured SMB network destination. Convert existing timelapse image sequences into video with the built-in converter.

Diagnostics and Benchmarks

Inspect advertised stream modes, camera controls and recording compatibility. Run a benchmark to assess supported acquisition options and review local results alongside the bundled, reviewed camera database. Troubleshooting summaries and exportable diagnostics reports help investigate errors and warnings.

Compatibility

Requires Android 8.0 or later. USB camera operation requires USB host/OTG support and a compatible UVC camera. Controls, resolutions, frame rates, recording formats and hardware encoding depend on the camera and Android device. Benchmark results describe the tested configurations.
