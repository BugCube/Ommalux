## 2. Camera images and recordings

Ommalux accesses a connected USB camera or, when enabled, a device camera to display a preview and create photographs, videos and timelapse sequences. Image processing, overlays, focus analysis and histogram calculations take place on your Android device. Camera access requires the relevant Android permission or USB authorization.

Photographs, videos and optional metadata are saved to the destination you configure. Metadata can include acquisition settings, timestamps and information you enter. Image content and entered metadata may contain personal or sensitive information, depending on what you record.

The current application has no microphone recording feature and requests no microphone permission.

## 3. Settings, profiles and camera information

Ommalux stores application preferences, camera-profile configurations and, according to your Remember Camera selection, camera preferences and local benchmark evidence in application storage. Camera identification includes information such as manufacturer, product name and USB vendor/product identifiers. Device-camera selection uses the Android camera identifier.

Locally saved profiles remain on your device until you delete them or remove application data. Exporting a profile creates a separate file in your selected storage destination. Exported files follow that destination's access and retention settings.

## 4. Storage destinations and network drives

You can save files on your device, through an Android document provider, or on a network drive you configure. A document provider may itself use a cloud service. The provider or network-drive operator handles files according to its own settings and privacy practices.

For network-drive access, Ommalux uses the server address, share, folder, username, password and authentication domain you supply. If you choose to save credentials, the password is encrypted using an Android Keystore key. Connection configuration is stored on the device. Network credentials are used to authenticate to your chosen server.

If a transfer fails or the network drive is unavailable, files may remain in application storage for later upload or recovery. These files consume local storage. Disconnecting a network drive can leave pending files on the device. Network protection depends on the server and negotiated SMB configuration, so use a trusted network or an appropriately secured remote connection.

## 5. Diagnostics and benchmarks

Diagnostics processes camera capabilities, control readbacks, stream and recording configurations, Android manufacturer/model and version, Ommalux version, encoder information, event timestamps and operational results. Camera benchmarks actively test available functions and may create temporary acquisition and storage-test files.

Event logs and acquisition histories are bounded and process-scoped. Local benchmark evidence may persist according to your camera-remembering choice. Exported diagnostic reports contain human-readable information and structured technical evidence, including benchmark results and retained histories.

Diagnostic export includes filtering intended to exclude passwords, authentication tokens, sensitive storage paths and unnecessary personal information. Review a report before sharing it. Filenames, user-entered content or attachments may themselves contain identifying information.

Temporary files may remain after interrupted operations or unsuccessful cleanup. Successful export can also create a private cached report copy for later attachment selection.

## 6. Contact, attachments and camera-database submissions

The Contact feature opens an email application with your selected subject and optional attachment. The email application receives access to the attachment when you invoke this action. Sending the message is controlled through that application.

Sending an enquiry or diagnostic report is voluntary. We use your email address to respond to your enquiry.

When you email us, we receive the sender information, message and attachments you send, together with associated email delivery information. We use this information to respond, investigate problems, discuss requests and review camera compatibility evidence. Email addressed to **ommalux@bugcube.de** is forwarded through **IONOS** to the developer’s **Telekom Freemail (@t-online.de)** mailbox provided by **Telekom Deutschland GmbH**. Both providers process the message as part of forwarding, delivery and mailbox services. Email delivery also involves the sender’s email provider.

Diagnostic-report attachments are downloaded to the developer’s computer and reviewed personally by the developer. Downloaded reports and working copies are subject to the retention period described below.

If you submit a report for Camera database verification, reviewed technical information may be included in the Global Camera Database distributed with Ommalux and in accompanying developer documentation. This can include camera model and identifiers, tested Android environment, application version, test configurations and outcomes. Sender contact details and unrelated personal content are excluded from published records. Database inclusion requires manual review.

## 7. Advertising, analytics and external services

The current build contains no integrated advertising, behavioral analytics or automatic developer crash-reporting service. It has no active account sign-in, subscription-purchase or Google Drive authorization flow. The policy will be updated if data handling changes when such functionality is introduced.

Opening external links, using document providers and invoking an email application involves those services and their privacy policies. Google Play and Android may independently process installation, purchase or platform diagnostic information under their own policies and your device settings.

## 8. Purposes and legal grounds

Ommalux processes data to provide the camera, recording, storage, profile and diagnostic functions you choose.

Where the GDPR applies, processing necessary to perform a contract with you or take pre-contractual steps at your request relies on Article 6(1)(b). General support, technical camera-verification review and operation of the information website rely on Article 6(1)(f). Our legitimate interests are responding to enquiries, maintaining Ommalux, documenting camera compatibility and making application information available securely. Processing required by applicable law relies on Article 6(1)(c). Where processing requires consent, we will request it and you may withdraw it for future processing.

## 9. Retention and deletion

Settings and internally stored profiles remain until removed through available application controls, Android's application-data controls or uninstallation. Clearing application data or uninstalling can also remove locally retained recovery files and pending uploads. Save any files you need first.

Files exported to device folders, document providers, network drives or email remain subject to those destinations' retention and deletion controls. Removing Ommalux does not delete those external copies. Process-scoped event logs and histories reset when the application process restarts.

We retain support correspondence and submitted diagnostic reports for as long as necessary to handle the enquiry and related follow-up. We review completed enquiries monthly and delete their correspondence, attachments and working copies within **90 days after closure**, unless a legal obligation requires longer retention.

An enquiry is closed when its handling and necessary follow-up are complete. We review inactive enquiries to determine whether further handling remains necessary. Deletion includes developer-controlled email copies, downloaded reports, analysis copies and copies retained in email Trash or the computer’s Recycle Bin. Where a legal obligation requires longer retention, we retain the required information for the applicable period.

Reviewed technical camera-database entries are retained separately while useful for documenting camera compatibility. These entries exclude sender names, email addresses and unrelated personal content. The support-correspondence retention rule applies to original submissions and their working copies. Database entries may continue to be distributed in Ommalux releases and accompanying documentation.

## 11. Your choices and rights

You can revoke camera and file-access permissions through Android, change storage and credential preferences, delete internal profiles, clear the local camera database, and manage exported files through their storage destination.

Where applicable law provides these rights, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or portability of personal information we hold, object to processing based on legitimate interests, or withdraw consent. Contact **ommalux@bugcube.de**. You may also complain to a competent data-protection supervisory authority. We may need sufficient information to identify the correspondence or submission concerned.

## 12. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when Ommalux's data handling changes. The published policy will state when it was last updated. Material changes requiring additional notice or consent will be handled before the relevant processing begins.
